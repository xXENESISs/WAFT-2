WAFT Combat Guide

Core combat systems, stats and biome rules

Combat Attributes

Each fighter is defined by 8 core attributes:

  • Life: Determines total health points in combat.
  • Attack: Determines base offensive damage.
  • Defense: Reduces incoming damage.
  • Resistance: Determines maximum stamina and fatigue tolerance.
  • Speed: Determines turn priority and slightly affects accuracy and evasion.
  • Agility: Main evasive stat.
  • Technique: Main precision stat and core of refined attacks.
  • Explosiveness: Determines critical potential and explosive attack power.

Life System

Life is converted directly into combat health points with a simple rule:

Total HP = Life × 10

Example: a fighter with 87 Life will enter combat with 870 HP.

Initiative

Speed determines who acts first each turn.

If one fighter has higher Speed, that fighter begins the turn unless:

  • the opponent uses Quick Attack, or
  • the opponent uses Concentration.

Damage Formula

D = Attack − Defense

Multiplier (%) = 75 + (D ÷ 4)

Damage = Attack × (Multiplier ÷ 100)

When Attack and Defense are equal, the attacker deals 75% of its Attack stat as damage.

This creates a balanced baseline where neither equal Attack nor equal Defense completely cancels the exchange.

Accuracy and Evasion

Precision = Technique × 0.9 + Speed × 0.1

Evasion = Agility × 0.9 + Speed × 0.1

D = Precision − Evasion

Base Hit Chance (%) = 60 + (D × 0.8)

Final rules:

  • If hit chance drops below 20%, uses 20%.
  • If hit chance rises above 100%, uses 100%.
  • If Precision and Evasion are equal, the base chance to hit is 60%.

Stamina and Fatigue

Maximum Stamina = Resistance × 10

Fatigue affects the following stats:

  • Damage
  • Precision
  • Evasion
  • Defense
  • Speed

Fatigue Thresholds

  • Above 50% stamina: no penalty
  • 50% stamina or below: −10%
  • 25% stamina or below: −20%
  • 0 stamina: −30%

Explosiveness and Criticals

Critical Chance (%) = Explosiveness × 0.4

Critical Damage = Final Damage × 1.5

Explosiveness is an offensive risk stat: it increases damage ceiling but not consistency.

Explosive Attack grants +20% critical chance on top of the base value.

Combat Actions

Normal Attack

Cost: 5 stamina

Precision: Technique × 0.9 + Speed × 0.1

Effect: no special bonus

Quick Attack

Cost: 15 stamina

Precision: Agility × 0.6 + Technique × 0.3 + Speed × 0.1

Effect: acts first

Precise Attack

Cost: 15 stamina

Precision: Technique × 0.9 + Speed × 0.1

Effect: +15% final hit chance

Explosive Attack

Cost: 20 stamina

Precision: Explosiveness × 0.6 + Technique × 0.3 + Speed × 0.1

Effect: +20% critical chance

Concentration

Cost: 0 stamina

Effects:

  • +10% Defense
  • +20 Stamina
  • +20 Life

Duration: 1 turn

The fighter does not attack that turn.

Biome System

Every fighter is assigned 6 biome affinities:

  • 2 favorable biomes
  • 2 neutral biomes
  • 2 unfavorable biomes

Before each combat:

  1. One biome is selected at random.
  2. One combat stat is selected at random.

The selected stat is modified depending on biome affinity:

  • Favorable: +10%
  • Neutral: 0%
  • Unfavorable: −10%

Life and Resistance are never affected by biome modifiers.

The biome can affect only these stats:

  • Attack
  • Defense
  • Speed
  • Agility
  • Technique
  • Explosiveness

Biomes

Arctic Desert Jungle Forest Marine Mountain

Combat Philosophy

WAFT is built around contrast, adaptation and identity. A fighter is not defined only by raw numbers, but by the relationship between creature biology, martial style, biome affinity and combat role. Some fighters dominate through precision, others through endurance, others through speed, explosiveness or situational advantage. The system is designed so that specialization matters.
